项目背景

Background

在海量播客内容中，用户很难发现真正适合自己的节目。现有平台的推荐算法以节目为单位，忽略了"主播个人"这一关键维度——同一个主播可能在多档节目里出现，而用户往往是因为喜欢某个人的声音和表达风格才持续收听的。

In a sea of podcast content, it's hard for listeners to discover shows that truly resonate. Existing recommendation algorithms focus on shows as a unit, overlooking a key dimension: the host themselves — the same host may appear across multiple shows, and users often follow because of a voice, a style, a personality.

解决方案

Solution

以"主播"为核心单元重构播客发现体验。关注你喜欢的主播，自动聚合他们参与的所有节目，跨平台、跨节目追踪同一个声音的更新。

Rebuild podcast discovery around the host as the core unit. Follow the hosts you love, automatically aggregate all shows they appear on, and track their voice across platforms and programs.

产品将主播作为一级实体，用户关注某位主播后，系统会自动抓取并整合该主播在所有平台、所有节目的内容更新，形成以人为中心的播客 Feed。

The product treats the host as a first-class entity. Once a user follows a host, the system automatically aggregates all their content updates across platforms and shows, building a host-centered podcast feed.

Led product design and presentation — built the interaction logic and visual interface from scratch, and delivered the full live demo on stage.
