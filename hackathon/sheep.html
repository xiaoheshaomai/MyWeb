主题与洞察

Theme & Insight

这次黑客松的主题叫 "The Most Silly Hackathon"。但我认为，silly 的东西也可以有洞察（insight）。

The hackathon theme was "The Most Silly Hackathon". But I believed: silly things can still carry real insight.

主办方提供的道具是一个 Goat Soundbox——按下不同按钮，它会发出各种羊叫声。拿到这个道具的瞬间，我想到了一个问题：

The prop provided was a Goat Soundbox — press different buttons and it makes all kinds of sheep sounds. The moment I held it, a question came to me:

有多少话，我们明明想说，却难以启齿？

How many things do we want to say — but can't find the courage to start?

难以启齿的话

Things We Can't Say

生活里有很多这样的瞬间——

Life is full of these moments —

🏫 课堂上

🏫 In Class

"老师……能不能少布置点作业？"

"Teacher… could you maybe assign a little less homework?"

🚇 地铁上

🚇 On the Subway

"你好，你叫什么名字？可以认识你吗？"

"Hi… what's your name? Can I get to know you?"

如果开口太难——那就让羊帮你说。

If words are too hard — let the sheep speak for you.

通过机器学习，我们提前对所有羊叫声按钮进行了分类，建立了一套"羊语密码系统"。发送方组合按钮输入心意，接收方通过 Goat Translator 一键破译。

Using machine learning, we pre-classified all sheep sound buttons — building a "sheep language cipher." The sender composes their message in sounds; the receiver decodes it with one tap on Goat Translator.

我的角色

My Role

除了产品设计，我还主导了现场展示。黑客松最重要的环节是上台 Demo——我希望评委和观众不只是听我讲，而是真正感受到这个产品的使用场景。

Beyond product design, I led the live presentation. The most important part of any hackathon is the demo — I wanted the judges and audience to feel the use case, not just hear about it.

我邀请了两位 mentor——Jasper 担任旁白，Leda 出演对象——一起现场表演了一个小品：

I invited our two mentors — Jasper as narrator, Leda as the stranger — to perform a live sketch:

在图书馆里，Rico 对 Leda 一见倾心，却始终羞于开口。她用 Sheep Translator 组合了一段羊叫声，悄悄送了出去。Leda 打开 Goat Translator，解码出"你好，你叫什么名字？"——两人相视而笑。整个过程极其清晰地还原了产品的使用方式，让全场会心一笑。

In a library, Rico has a crush on Leda but is too shy to speak. She composes a sheep sound message and sends it over. Leda opens Goat Translator, decodes "Hi… what's your name?" — and they both smile. The sketch made the product's use case instantly vivid. We took 1st place.

Sheep Translator — Hackathon Script

Characters: Narrator (Jasper) · Rico · Leda

[Scene 1]

(Projector shows slides)

Narrator

Rico is a very shy girl.

Every day, she sees Leda in the library…

And every day, she wants to talk to her.

But she never has the courage.

[Scene 2]

Narrator

One day, an idea came to her mind.

Rico

I can use the sheep sounds to say hi!

(Projector changes to Sheep Translator interface)

Narrator

So Rico opened Sheep Translator.

She typed: "Hi… what's your name?"

Then she recorded her own sheep sound.

[Scene 3]

Narrator

The next day, Rico finally stopped being shy.

She walked up to Leda… and played the sheep sound.

Speaker / Sound

"Baaaaaaaaaaaa~"

(Pause)

Leda

(suddenly realizes) Wait… I can use Sheep Translator!

[Scene 4]

Narrator

Leda opened Sheep Translator.

(Projector shows translation result: "Hi… what's your name?")

Leda

Oh! Hi, I'm Leda.

Do you want to go to a farm together and listen to sheep sounds?

[Ending]

Narrator

And that… was the beginning of their story.


Sometimes people are not afraid of talking.
They are just afraid of making the first sound.
